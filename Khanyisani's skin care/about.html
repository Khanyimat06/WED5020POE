Khanyisani's Skin Care was born from a simple truth: everyone deserves to feel confident in their own skin. "Khanyisani" means "to bring light" - and that's our promise to you.

That's why we carefully formulated and selected products that remove pimples, deeply hydrate dry skin, brighten your tone, and restore your natural glow. Skincare made for our weather, our water, and all South African skin tones.

How To Use Our Products

Great skin comes from a simple routine. Here's how to get the best results:

1. Face Scrub: Wet your face with lukewarm water. Apply a small amount of scrub and massage in gentle circles for 1 minute. Focus on areas with blackheads and pimples. Rinse well. Use 2-3 times a week.
2. Hydrating Cream / Moisturizer: After washing your face, apply to slightly damp skin morning and night. This locks in moisture and keeps dry skin soft all day.
3. Facial Oil / Serum: Use 2-3 drops on clean skin at night. Gently press into skin. It helps fade dark spots and gives you that healthy Khanyisani glow while you sleep.
4. Cleanser: Use morning and night. Massage onto wet skin, then rinse. It removes dirt and oil without stripping your skin.
